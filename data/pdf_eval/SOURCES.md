# Evaluation set: sources and licences

10 PDFs and 61 questions (`eval_dataset.jsonl`), small enough to evaluate the whole chain in a few minutes of
parsing. No Open RAG Bench content: its CC BY-NC licence forbids it in a commercial kit.
`tests/test_eval_set.py` checks that every evidence string is in its PDF and every section is a real heading.

## Synthetic documents (9)

Generated by `scripts/make_eval_pdfs.py`. Fictitious organisations, places and figures; no licence restriction.
`expected.json` gives their real headings.

## arXiv paper (1), CC BY 4.0

Downloaded by `scripts/fetch_eval_arxiv.py`, checked by SHA-256, not committed. The questions are ours.

* Jonas Esser, Mateus Maia, Andrew C. Parnell, Judith E. Bosmans, Johanna M. van Dongen, Thomas Klausch, Keefe Murphy.
  *Seemingly unrelated Bayesian additive regression trees for cost-effectiveness analyses in healthcare.*
  arXiv:2404.02228v3, https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.02228, licence CC BY 4.0. Its headings are run into their
  paragraphs and Docling reads them as text; its PDF bookmarks give them back (`HGRAG_OUTLINE_HEADINGS`). One of its
  headings is a 180-word paragraph that Docling took for a heading (`HGRAG_MAX_HEADING_WORDS`).

The PDF is unchanged. Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

## Known weak points the set measures

* 02: run-in headings without bookmarks stay inside their paragraph (q07 to q11 expect real sections).
* 04: values readable only on a chart, answered through the description of the vision model (q18 to q20).
* 08: formulas read through the vision model (q42, q44).
* 09: headings in the body size: Docling may read them as text.
